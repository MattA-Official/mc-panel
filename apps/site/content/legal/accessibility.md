We want as many people as possible to be able to use the Flytower website at `https://flytower.example`,
whatever their device, connection or ability. This statement says what we are aiming for, how far we
have got, and how to tell us when something does not work for you.

## What we are aiming for

Our target is the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) at
level AA. In practice that means you should be able to:

- use the whole site with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator and a link to skip to the
  main content;
- use it with a screen reader, with pages that have sensible headings, landmarks, link text and
  alternative text for meaningful images;
- zoom to 400% without content being cut off or needing to scroll in two directions;
- read text that has enough contrast against its background, in both the light and the dark theme;
- choose a light or dark theme, or follow your device's setting;
- browse without animation if your device is set to reduce motion;
- understand status information without relying on colour alone;
- read the documentation without JavaScript being essential for the content itself.

We also try to write in plain English, and to explain technical terms the first time they appear.

## The Flytower software

This statement covers the website. The Flytower panel that you install on your own server is built with
the same aims and the same components, and accessibility problems in it are treated as bugs. Please
report them in the same way.

## Tell us about a problem

If you find something you cannot use, or you need information from this website in a different format,
please tell us:

- email [hello@flytower.example](mailto:hello@flytower.example);
- or open an issue in the [source repository](https://github.com/MattA-Official/mc-panel/issues), if
  you are comfortable posting publicly.

It helps if you tell us the page address, what you were trying to do, and the browser and any assistive
technology you were using. Flytower is maintained by one person, so we aim to reply within 10 working
days, and we will tell you what we can do and by when.

## If you are not happy with our response

We take our duties under the Equality Act 2010 seriously. If you contact us and are not satisfied with
the answer, you can get advice from the
[Equality Advisory and Support Service](https://www.equalityadvisoryservice.com/). In Northern Ireland,
contact the [Equality Commission for Northern Ireland](https://www.equalityni.org/).

## Changes to this statement

We will review this statement at least once a year, and whenever the site's design changes
