Flytower is free. To help pay for the time and running costs behind it, the website may carry a small
amount of advertising and some referral links, alongside [sponsorship](/sponsor). This policy explains
exactly how that works, so you always know when money is involved.

## Our commitments

1. **No advertising in the software.** The Flytower panel you install will never show adverts or
   referral links. Advertising appears only on this website.
2. **Always labelled.** Every advert, sponsor placement and referral link is clearly marked before you
   click it.
3. **No tracking.** Adverts on this website do not use cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or
   profiles of what you do. They are chosen to suit the page, never the person.
4. **Editorial independence.** Nobody can pay to change what the documentation says, what the software
   does, or what we recommend.
5. **Not much of it.** Advertising is limited to a small number of fixed positions. There are no
   pop-ups, no auto-playing video or sound, and no adverts dressed up as content or as download
   buttons.

## What is on the website today

- **An "Advertise here" placeholder.** At launch there is no advertising network and no paid advert.
  The advertising position shows a placeholder that links to information for advertisers.
- **No referral links yet.** When pages that suggest hosting providers are added, some of their links
  will be referral links, labelled as described below.
- **Sponsor recognition**, in sections labelled as sponsors. See the
  [Sponsorship and donations terms](/legal/sponsorship).

## Referral links

Some links to hosting providers, such as virtual private server (VPS) companies, may be referral links,
also known as affiliate links. If you sign up after following one, Matt may receive a commission or
account credit from the provider. It costs you nothing extra, and some referral links give you a
discount or free credit as well.

- **How they are marked.** Each referral link has the words "referral link" beside it, and any page
  that contains referral links says so near the top. You will know before you click.
- **You never have to use one.** You can go to the provider's own website directly instead, and
  everything in the documentation will work the same way.
- **How providers are chosen.** We only suggest providers we have used ourselves or have good reason to
  believe suit running a Minecraft server. A provider cannot pay to be included or to be placed higher.
  Where we mention a provider that has no referral programme, we say so in the same way as any other.
- **Not a guarantee.** A referral link is a suggestion, not a promise about a provider's price,
  performance or reliability. Prices and offers change, so check the provider's own site. Your contract
  is with the provider.
- **Privacy.** Following a referral link tells the provider that you came from Flytower. The provider
  may then set cookies on its own site under its own privacy policy. We are told that a referral
  happened and what it earned, not who you are. See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

For search engines, referral links are marked with `rel="sponsored"`.

## Advertising on the website

### Adverts arranged directly with us

An advertiser may book the advertising position directly. These adverts:

- are a static image or text with a link, served from our own website;
- carry no advertiser scripts, tracking pixels or cookies;
- are labelled "Advertisement" or "Ad", and their links are marked with `rel="sponsored"`.

### An advertising network, in future

Later, we may fill the advertising position through an advertising network. If we do, it will be a
contextual network that selects adverts by the content of the page, does not use cookies, and does not
track or profile visitors. We are considering EthicalAds and Carbon Ads. Before any network is switched
on, we will update this policy and the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) to name it and explain what
data it handles.

### What we will not advertise

We will not accept adverts, sponsors or referral arrangements for:

- anything illegal in the UK, or anything misleading, including fake download buttons and false claims;
- gambling, betting, lotteries or loot-box style mechanics;
- alcohol, tobacco, vaping, weapons, or adult content;
- cryptocurrency, NFTs, trading platforms or get-rich-quick schemes;
- high-cost credit, or other financial products we are not able to assess;
- malware, cheats, hacked clients, account sellers, or tools for attacking servers;
- Minecraft servers or services that break the
  [Minecraft End User Licence Agreement](https://www.minecraft.net/eula) or Mojang's usage guidelines,
  such as selling gameplay advantages in ways those rules forbid;
- political or religious campaigning;
- anything unsuitable for a website that younger people may visit.

We may refuse or remove any advert for any reason.

### Standards for advertisers

All advertising must be legal, decent, honest and truthful, and must follow the UK Code of Non-broadcast
Advertising and Direct & Promotional Marketing (the CAP Code) and UK consumer law. The advertiser is
responsible for its advert, for the claims in it, and for holding the rights to everything in it.

### Information for advertisers

To ask about advertising, email [hello@flytower.example](mailto:hello@flytower.example). In outline:

- price, position, dates and the advert itself are agreed in writing by email before anything runs;
- we report only what our cookieless analytics can show, which is aggregate figures such as page views
  for the pages the advert appeared on. We cannot provide tracking pixels, audience data or
  per-visitor reporting, and we do not guarantee impressions, clicks or sales;
- we may remove an advert that breaks this policy, and will refund the unused part of the booking
  unless the advertiser misled us about it;
- buying advertising does not buy coverage, recommendations, or influence over the project.

## Sponsors, adverts and referrals: the difference

|                     | What it is                                                         | How you can tell                         |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Sponsor**         | Someone supporting the project, who may be thanked by name or logo | Appears in a section labelled "Sponsors" |
| **Advert**          | A paid placement promoting a product or service                    | Labelled "Advertisement" or "Ad"         |
| **Referral link**   | A link that may earn Matt a commission if you sign up              | Has "referral link" beside it            |
| **Everything else** | Our own content. Nobody paid for it or approved it.                | No label                                 |

## Complaints

If you think an advert or referral link on this website is misleading, is not labelled clearly, or
breaks this policy, please email [hello@flytower.example](mailto:hello@flytower.example) and we will
look into it. You can also complain to the
[Advertising Standards Authority](https://www.asa.org.uk/make-a-complaint.html).
