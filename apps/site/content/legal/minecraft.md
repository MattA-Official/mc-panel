## What that means

Flytower is an independent, open source tool for managing Minecraft servers that you host yourself. It
is made by one person, Matt Adcock, and is not made, endorsed, sponsored or supported by Mojang AB or
Microsoft Corporation.

"Minecraft" is a trademark of Mojang AB. We use the name only to describe what Flytower works with, in
line with Mojang's [Minecraft Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/usage-guidelines). The
Flytower name, logo and design are our own and are deliberately not based on Minecraft's branding or
artwork.

## Flytower does not include Minecraft

Flytower does not contain or redistribute any Minecraft software. When you create a server, Flytower
downloads the server software directly from its official source, onto your own machine, at your
request.

To run a Minecraft server you must accept the
[Minecraft End User Licence Agreement](https://www.minecraft.net/eula). Flytower asks you to confirm
that you accept it and never accepts it for you. You, as the person running the server, are responsible
for following the EULA and the usage guidelines, including their rules about how servers may make
money.

Players need their own legitimate copy of Minecraft to join your server. Flytower does not provide
game accounts and does not help anyone get around Minecraft's licensing or authentication.

## Other projects and trademarks

Flytower can install and update software and content from independent projects and services, including
Fabric, PaperMC, Hangar, Modrinth and Vanilla Tweaks, and it uses Java builds from Eclipse Adoptium.
This website also mentions services such as GitHub, Discord, Ko-fi, Cloudflare and various hosting
providers.

All of these names and logos belong to their respective owners. We use them only to identify the
projects and services concerned. Unless we say otherwise, Flytower is not affiliated with, endorsed by
or sponsored by any of them.

Mods, plugins and datapacks are written by their own authors and come under their own licences.
Flytower downloads them from the source you choose and does not claim any rights in them. Their authors
are responsible for them, and you are responsible for checking that you are allowed to use them on your
server.
