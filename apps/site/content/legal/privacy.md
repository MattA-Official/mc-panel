This policy explains what personal data is collected when you use the Flytower website at
`https://flytower.example`, sponsor the project, or get in touch, and what happens to that data.

The short version:

- The website sets **no cookies** and has **no accounts, no advertising trackers and no mailing list**.
- It uses cookieless, aggregate analytics so we can see which pages are useful. You can opt out.
- The Flytower software you install on your own server has **no telemetry**. Your server's data stays
  on your server.
- We never sell personal data.

## What this policy covers, and what it does not

It covers this website, emails you send us, sponsorship and donations, advertising enquiries, and the
project's community spaces as far as we control them.

It does **not** cover a Flytower panel that somebody else runs. Flytower is self-hosted software. The
person or organisation running a panel decides what it stores about their players and staff, and they
are the controller for that data. We have no access to it. If you play on a server managed with
Flytower and have a question about your data, ask whoever runs that server. See
[The Flytower software](#the-flytower-software) below.

## The data we collect and why

### When you visit the website

| Data                                                                                               | Why we use it                                                           | Lawful basis                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| IP address, browser and device details, the page requested, and the date and time                  | To deliver the site to you, keep it secure, and block abuse and attacks | Legitimate interests (running a secure, reliable site)   |

The website is hosted on Cloudflare. Every request passes through Cloudflare's network, which processes
your IP address and request details on our behalf in order to serve the page and protect the site. We
do not build profiles from this data or try to identify visitors.

Analytics are provided by Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not use cookies, does not store anything on
your device, and does not fingerprint you or follow you across other websites. We only see totals, such
as how many people read a page. We cannot see what any one visitor did.

### Cookies and similar technologies

The website sets no cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

The only thing it stores on your device is your theme choice (light, dark or system), which is kept in
your browser's local storage so the site looks the same on your next visit. It is never sent to us. You
can remove it by clearing site data in your browser.

If you follow a link to another service, such as GitHub, Ko-fi, Discord or a hosting provider, that
service may set its own cookies under its own policy.

### When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you give it, and whatever you write. We use
this to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in
answering people who contact us. Please do not send sensitive information that we have not asked for.

### When you sponsor or donate

Sponsorship and donations are handled by GitHub Sponsors and Ko-fi. Those platforms take the payment,
and they are independent controllers of the data you give them. **We never see or store your card or
bank details.**

What the platforms pass on to us depends on the platform and on your own settings there:

- **GitHub Sponsors:** your GitHub username and public profile, your tier or amount, the dates of your
  sponsorship, whether you chose to be public or private, and your email address only if you opt in to
  sharing it.
- **Ko-fi:** the name you enter, your email address, the amount, and any message you leave.

We use this data to:

- thank you and provide any recognition that comes with your tier, such as listing your name or logo.
  The basis is that it is necessary to do what we promised under the
  [Sponsorship and donations terms](/legal/sponsorship);
- keep the income and tax records that UK law requires. The basis is a legal obligation;
- understand, in total, how the project is funded. The basis is our legitimate interest in planning the
  project.

We only list you publicly as a sponsor if you chose to be public on the platform, or you have told us
you want to be listed. You can ask to be removed at any time.

### When you ask about advertising

If you email us about advertising on the website, we keep your business contact details and our
correspondence so we can discuss, arrange and invoice the placement. The basis is taking steps towards,
and then performing, a contract with you or your organisation, plus our legal obligation to keep
business records.

### Community spaces

The project's source code, issues and discussions are on GitHub, and community chat is on Discord. Those
services run under their own terms and privacy policies, and each is the controller of your account
data. As maintainer and moderator, Matt can see what you post there and the public profile you post it
under, and may moderate it. Anything you post in public issues, discussions or channels can be read by
anyone, so do not post personal data, passwords, server addresses you want kept private, or secrets
from your configuration.

### Referral links

Some links to hosting providers are referral links. They are always labelled. If you follow one, the
provider learns that you came from Flytower, and may set its own cookies on its own site. If you sign
up, we may receive a commission or account credit. Providers tell us that a referral happened and what
it earned. They do not normally tell us who you are. See the
[Advertising and referrals policy](/legal/advertising).

Cloudflare, GitHub, Ko-fi and Discord operate internationally, so your data may be processed outside

that applies to a particular provider.

## How we keep data safe

The website has no accounts and no database of visitors, which is the best protection there is. For
the data we do hold, we use reputable providers, strong unique passwords with two-factor authentication,
and we keep access to Matt alone. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach were likely to put your
rights at risk, we would tell you and the Information Commissioner's Office as the law requires.

## Children

The website is written for people who run Minecraft servers. It is not aimed at children, and we do not
knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Because younger people are interested in
Minecraft, the site is designed to be safe for everyone by default: no accounts, no profiling, no
behavioural advertising and no chat on the site itself. If you believe a child has sent us personal
data, email us and we will delete it.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

- **be informed** about how your data is used, which is the purpose of this policy;
- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **have it corrected** if it is wrong or incomplete;
- **have it erased** in some circumstances;
- **restrict** how we use it in some circumstances;
- **object** to processing that relies on legitimate interests, including analytics;
- **data portability** for data you gave us that we process by automated means under a contract or with
  your consent;
- **withdraw consent** at any time where we rely on consent. Withdrawing it does not affect anything
  done before.

To use any of these rights, email [privacy@flytower.example](mailto:privacy@flytower.example). There is
no charge. We will reply within one month. We may need to check who you are before acting, and we will
only ask for what we need to do that.

For data held by GitHub, Ko-fi or Discord about your account with them, you will usually need to ask
that service directly.

If you live in the European Economic Area, you have equivalent rights under the EU GDPR.

## The Flytower software

Flytower is software that you download and run on your own server. This section describes how it is
designed, so that you can make your own privacy decisions when you run it.

- **No telemetry.** The software does not send us usage statistics, crash reports, server names, player
  names or any other data about you or your server.
- **Your data stays with you.** Accounts, passkeys, player lists, logs, backups and settings are stored
  on your own server, or in storage that you configure, such as your own backup bucket.
- **It does make some outbound connections,** all to do the job you asked for. It checks GitHub for new
  Flytower releases. It downloads server software, mods, plugins, datapacks and Java from the sources
  you choose, such as Mojang, Fabric, PaperMC, Modrinth, Hangar, Vanilla Tweaks and Adoptium. It may
  look up player names with Mojang's public API. It sends notifications and backups only to the
  destinations you set up. Each of those services sees your server's IP address and handles it under its
  own privacy policy.
- **You are the controller for your server.** If you run a server for other people, you decide what is
  collected about your players and staff, such as usernames, IP addresses in server logs and chat, and
  you are responsible for telling them and for following the law where you are. We are not a processor
  for you, because nothing is sent to us.

## Things we might add later

We have built the website so that these could be added. None of them exists today, and we will update
this policy **before** any of them is switched on.

- **A mailing list.** It would be opt-in only. You would confirm your address by email before receiving
  anything, every email would have an unsubscribe link, and your address would be used for nothing else.
- **Opt-in telemetry in the software.** It would be off by default, switched on only by the server
  owner, limited to anonymous technical information such as version numbers, and documented field by
  field. It would never include player data.
- **An advertising network on the website.** It would be a contextual network that does not use cookies
  or track people. The [Advertising and referrals policy](/legal/advertising) explains the standards we
  hold ads to.
