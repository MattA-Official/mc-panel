Thank you for thinking about supporting Flytower. These terms explain what a sponsorship or donation
is, what it is not, and what you can expect from us. They apply to every payment made to support the
project, whether monthly or one-off.

## Who you are supporting

Flytower is an open source project run by one person, Matt Adcock, who is based in England. In these
terms, "we", "us" and "Flytower" mean Matt.

Sponsorships and donations are paid to Matt **personally**. Flytower is not a company and it is **not a
charity**. That means:

- your payment is not a charitable donation, Gift Aid cannot be claimed on it, and it is unlikely to be
  tax-deductible for you;
- Matt declares the money he receives as income and pays any tax due on it;
- Flytower is not registered for VAT, so no VAT is charged by us and we cannot issue a VAT invoice.

## What a sponsorship or donation is

It is a voluntary contribution towards the time and running costs of an open source project. We are
grateful for every one.

It is **not**:

- a purchase of the software. Flytower is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0, and it stays that way
  for everyone whether or not they pay anything;
- a support contract. It does not come with guaranteed support, response times, uptime, or fixes;
- a way to buy features. We listen to sponsors, as we do to everyone, but sponsoring does not give you
  control over the roadmap, priority for your requests, or a say in project decisions;
- an investment. You get no ownership, shares, or right to any return;
- an endorsement. Listing a sponsor does not mean we recommend their products or services.

One-off donations come with no perks at all, only our thanks.

## Recognition for sponsors

Some tiers come with recognition, such as your name in a list of sponsors or your logo on the website.
The recognition for each tier is described on the [sponsor page](/sponsor) and on the platform. If the
two ever disagree, the platform's description at the time you signed up is the one that counts.

- We only list you publicly if you chose to be public on the platform, or you ask us to.
- We will add your recognition within a reasonable time, normally within 14 days of your sponsorship
  starting or of you sending us what we need, such as a logo.
- You confirm that you have the right to use any name, logo and link you give us, and you allow us to
  display them on the website and in the repository for as long as your sponsorship lasts.
- We may decline or remove any name, logo or link that we believe is unlawful, misleading, offensive,
  unsuitable for a site that younger people may visit, or contrary to the standards in our
  [Advertising and referrals policy](/legal/advertising).
- Sponsor links are marked as sponsored links for search engines. Sponsor logos appear in a section
  that is clearly labelled as sponsors.
- Recognition lasts while your sponsorship is active. We will remove it within a reasonable time after
  it ends, or sooner if you ask.

We may change the tiers and what they include for the future. If a change reduces what your current
tier includes, we will tell you through the platform or on the website before it takes effect, so you
can decide whether to continue.

## Cancelling

You can cancel a monthly sponsorship at any time through the platform. There is no minimum term and no
cancellation fee. The platform's rules decide exactly when the cancellation takes effect, which is
normally at the end of the period you have already paid for.

## Refunds

Sponsorships and donations are voluntary and are not normally refundable. We do not want anyone to be
out of pocket by mistake, so:

- if you ask within **14 days** of a payment, we will refund it, whatever the reason;
- after 14 days, tell us if something went wrong, such as a duplicate payment or the wrong amount, and
  we will do what we reasonably can to put it right.

Email [hello@flytower.example](mailto:hello@flytower.example) with the platform, the date and the
amount. Refunds are made through the platform you paid with, so they follow that platform's process and
timing, and the platform may not return its own fees. Nothing here affects any legal right you have to
cancel or to a refund.

## Our right to decline

We may decline or refund a sponsorship or donation, and end any recognition, if we believe that
accepting it would be unlawful, would harm the project's reputation or independence, or comes with
conditions we cannot accept. If we do that for a payment you have already made for a period that has
not yet started or finished, we will refund the unused part.

## If the project changes

We hope to work on Flytower for a long time, but it is maintained by one person and we cannot promise
that development will continue at any particular pace, or at all. If the project is paused or ended,
we will say so publicly and close the sponsorship tiers so that no further payments are taken.
